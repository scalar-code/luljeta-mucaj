Aboutthe artist

The daughter of the painter and professor Kadrush Rama, Luljeta grew up surrounded by art. Her work combines graphic design with painting, typography, fragments and nature. She often works in the rain and lets the weather become part of the piece.

Since 2009 she has taught visual arts at “Ismail Qemali” school in Prishtina. Since 2023 she has also lectured in Visual Communication at the University of Prishtina.

Aboutwho she is

Artist, designer, teacher and researcher.

Luljeta Rama Muçaj is a visual artist, graphic designer and lecturer from Prishtina, with more than twenty years in art education and contemporary practice.

Story

Luljeta grew up in Prishtina, the second of four children in a family of two professors. Her father, Kadrush Rama, is a painter known in Kosovo and abroad, and his influence shaped the education and creativity of all his children.

In 1996 she began studying Graphic Design at the Academy of Arts, University of Prishtina, and graduated in 2003. From 1999 to 2004 she lived in the United States. In New York she assisted with visual arts teaching at a private school and worked in two graphic design studios and print shops.

Back in Prishtina she taught art at the International School of Pristina. She completed a Master's in Graphic Design at the University of Prishtina in 2008. Since 2025 she has been a PhD candidate in History of Art and Archaeology at Ss. Cyril and Methodius University (UKIM) in Skopje, mentored by Prof. Dr. Antonio Jakimoski.

“Art is the voice of the soul, speaking in colors beyond words.” Luljeta Rama Muçaj
Practiceoften in the rain

She combines graphic design with painting, type and nature.

  • Painting

    Abstract and mixed-media painting, with techniques and textures inspired by natural phenomena. She often works outdoors in the rain.

  • Graphic design

    Conceptual graphic design, typography and posters.

  • Photography & objects

    Photography and mixed-media objects built from fragments.

Recurring themes: identity, memory, voice and silence, soil and roots, dialogue, and structures of mediation.

Teaching20+ years

Twenty years of teaching children and students to make art.

Luljeta has taught art since 2004, from preschool classes to university students. She teaches visual arts at “Ismail Qemali” school and lectures at the Faculty of Arts, University of Prishtina.

Now

  • Basics of DesignUniversity of Prishtina · since Oct 2023

    Design fundamentals for students of the Department of Dramaturgy.

  • Visual arts, grades 6–9“Ismail Qemali” school · since Jan 2009

    Theory, practice and assessment, lesson planning and working with parents.

Approachin her words

Nurture curiosity and encourage artistic expression.

  • Open horizons

    Show students what art can be, so they find creative paths they hadn't imagined.

  • Build focus

    Use new methods, materials and techniques, drawn from international research on children's art education.

  • Courage

    Help students look at life with courage and creativity, and enjoy what they achieve.

Students' workabroad

Through the international foundation Colours of Peace, her students' work has been shown in Dubai (2019), Ecuador (2020–2022), Rome (2021–2022) and at a museum in Barcelona (2022). In 2020 one of her students placed third among representatives of 145 countries in Ecuador. In 2023 the foundation appointed her its Ambassador of Kosovo.

In 2023 she twinned her school with the Liceo Artistico in Valenza, Italy. She also organises school exhibitions and has made local and national TV programmes, including on RTK, about talented students and their work.
